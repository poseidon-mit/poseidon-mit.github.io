# Nano Banana Pro Image Prompts for Poseidon Video

Based on the `videoresearch.docx` document, here are the optimized Nano Banana Pro (or Gemini 3 Pro Image) prompts to generate the "Visual DNA" and storyboard frames for the 60-second Poseidon Premium Fintech video.

The design language incorporates:
- **Base Color:** Deep Navy (`#0B132B`) / Jet Black (`#111111`)
- **Accent Color:** Neon Cyan (`#12B5CB`) / Electric Blue (`#1A73E8`)
- **Style:** Glassmorphism, minimalist, premium fintech, isometric 3D
- **Lighting:** Cinematic rim lighting, studio lighting, high contrast

---

### Scene 1: The Hook (0:00 - 0:10)
**Concept:** Real-world data extraction.
* **Prompt:** A macro close-up of a sleek, dark metallic physical city landscape where glowing cyan light particles and data points are rapidly being extracted from the surfaces. Deep navy blue background, electric blue accents, cinematic rim lighting, hyper-realistic 8k render, high contrast, premium tech aesthetic.
* **Negative Prompt:** organic elements, bright warm colors, messy background, text, watermarks, low resolution, cartoon.

### Scene 2: The Agitation (0:10 - 0:25)
**Concept:** Chaotic data and access blocked (legal risks).
* **Prompt:** Chaotic, unpredictable glowing data streams flowing in a dark void. In the center, a sleek frosted glassmorphism UI panel displaying a subtle, elegant red warning geometric icon indicating blocked access. Frosted glass textures with subtle light refractions, matte dark metal, deep navy blue background, cinematic studio lighting, premium fintech aesthetic.
* **Negative Prompt:** realistic humans, messy colors, organic elements, bright warm colors, cluttered background, low resolution, excessive text.

### Scene 3: The Solution (0:25 - 0:40)
**Concept:** Poseidon brings order to chaos.
* **Prompt:** A sleek, glowing minimalist 3D trident logo floating elegantly. Chaotic glowing cyan data streams around it smoothly transforming and aligning into perfectly ordered, crystal-clear glass blockchain cubes. Deep jet black background, neon cyan and electric blue light refractions, caustics on the edges, volumetric lighting, premium high-end tech aesthetic, isometric 3D perspective.
* **Negative Prompt:** messy background, text, watermarks, organic elements, bright warm colors, low resolution, muddy colors.

### Scene 4: The Validation (0:40 - 0:50)
**Concept:** Smart contract reward and data validation.
* **Prompt:** A clean, minimalist frosted glassmorphism UI card floating in a vast dark void. The glass card displays a luminous green checkmark indicating validation, with smooth electric blue digital streams distributing elegantly downwards. Matte dark metal edge details, subtle light refractions, deep jet black background, cinematic rim lighting, 8k high-end product photography style.
* **Negative Prompt:** messy background, text, watermarks, organic elements, bright warm colors, low resolution, cluttered UI.

### Scene 5: Call to Action (0:50 - 0:60)
**Concept:** Outro background for text placement.
* **Prompt:** A vast, minimalist deep navy blue void with a subtle, elegant glowing neon cyan circular pulse in the center, leaving ample clean empty space in the middle for typography. Cinematic rim lighting, soft light falloff, high contrast, frosted glass textures fading into the periphery, premium fintech brand aesthetic, clean and pure.
* **Negative Prompt:** messy background, text, watermarks, organic elements, bright warm colors, low resolution, complex structures.
