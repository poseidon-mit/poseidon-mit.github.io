## 7. Acceptance Tests (MIT Presentation Grade)

1. **Room presence**: first 2 seconds projected on a large screen must command full attention — no gradual warm-up.
2. **Wave identity**: the wave structure must be immediately legible as AI/neural architecture, not natural water.
3. **Scale**: viewer must feel the wave is impossibly large — IMAX-scale, not aquarium-scale.
4. **Loop quality**: no visible seam in 20 continuous loops at 100% playback speed.
5. **Text legibility**: white title text overlaid in top-third of frame must remain readable throughout all 6 seconds.
6. **Browser behavior**: `autoplay loop muted playsinline` verified in Chrome, Safari, Firefox.
7. **Performance**: compressed output ≤15MB for LP delivery.
