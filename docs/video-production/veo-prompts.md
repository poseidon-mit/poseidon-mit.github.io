# Veo 3.1 — 6-Layer Cinematic Video Generation Prompts

> Confirmed 9-scene script. Each scene has a 6-layer prompt (videoresearch.docx §6.1)
> plus an Image-to-Video motion prompt (§6.2).
>
> **Workflow**: Upload the scene's Visual DNA reference image → paste the I2V prompt → generate.
> For scenes without a reference image, use the full Text-to-Video prompt.

## Scene 1: The Hook — Deep Ocean Void (0:00–0:08, Beats 1–16)

**Narration**: "What if your money could actually think? Not fragmented tools. A unified intelligence seeing every dimension of your wealth."
**Camera**: Slow push-in | **Transition OUT**: Crossfade

### Text-to-Video (Full 6-Layer)

```
[Cinematography] Extreme wide shot. Static camera for the first 2 seconds, then an imperceptibly slow push-in begins (5% travel over 6 seconds). 50mm prime lens, f/2.0. Deep depth of field — the vast void is uniformly dark. Locked tripod feel. Anamorphic subtle barrel.

[Subject] Abstract frosted glass shapes of varying sizes, floating aimlessly in a deep dark-water void. The shapes are neutral — no engine color yet. They have a faint internal luminescence but are not activated. Subtle particles suspended around them like deep-sea plankton.

[Action] The glass shapes drift slowly and independently — rotating on different axes at different speeds. Particles float outward from the shapes in all directions with organic, decelerated motion. Over 8 seconds, the shapes' internal glow brightens by approximately 15%, suggesting something is about to change. Cool blue-white volumetric light from the top-left intensifies gradually.

[Context] Pure deep navy void (#020410). No ground plane, no horizon, no environmental elements. An infinite dark space. The only light sources are the glass shapes' internal glow and the top-left volumetric fill.

[Lighting & Atmosphere] Cool blue-white key light from top-left at 45 degrees. No engine-colored rim light yet (that comes in Scene 3). The atmosphere is "dormant potential in the deep ocean." Subtle volumetric scattering. Film grain 2%. Color temperature: 7000K cool.

[Negative] No people, no text, no bright backgrounds, no warm colors, no rapid movement, no shakiness, no lens flare, no dust, no smoke, no visible floor or walls, no neon signs.
```

### Image-to-Video (Motion-Only)

```
The camera holds still for 2 seconds, then begins an imperceptibly slow push-in. Frosted glass shapes drift slowly and independently, rotating on different axes. Particles float outward with organic motion. Internal glow brightens gradually by 15%. Cool blue-white volumetric light intensifies from top-left. Smooth, stable, no sudden changes. 8 seconds.
```

## Scene 2: The Problem — Fragmented Coordination (0:08–0:18, Beats 17–36)

**Narration**: "The coordination gap costs us dearly. Twelve point five billion lost to fraud. Twelve billion wasted in fees. Disconnected systems leave you exposed."
**Camera**: Slight dolly back, off-axis | **Transition OUT**: Dissolve with color shift

### Text-to-Video (Full 6-Layer)

```
[Cinematography] Medium-wide shot. Slow dolly back and slightly off-center (3% rightward drift over 8 seconds). 85mm lens, f/1.8 — the telephoto compression emphasizes the disconnection between elements. Extremely shallow DOF — edge elements are soft, center barely sharp.

[Subject] Frosted glass panels fracturing and separating. The glass fragments are irregularly shaped — previously unified panels now splitting apart. Chaotic intersecting lines between them blur in the shallow DOF. Subtle abstract data patterns on the glass surfaces flicker and distort.

[Action] The glass panels drift further apart over 8 seconds, accelerating slightly. Red (#EF4444) and amber (#F59E0B) caustic light bleeds between the fragments, intensifying as the scene progresses. Every 2-3 seconds, a panel dims momentarily (opacity drops 30% then recovers). The intersecting lines between panels break and disconnect. The ambient red-amber light strengthens from upper-right.

[Context] Deep navy void (#020410). Harsh amber-red warning light from the upper-right corner. The void feels vast and cold — the panels are lost in it. No ground plane.

[Lighting & Atmosphere] Desaturated, cold ambient fill. Red and amber caustics create subtle danger cues. The key light shifts from cool blue-white to warm amber-red, suggesting increasing threat. Film grain 3% (slightly higher for tension). The mood is "elegant decay — sophisticated systems failing silently."

[Negative] No people, no text, no bright backgrounds, no happy colors, no symmetry, no stability, no explosions, no fire.
```

### Image-to-Video (Motion-Only)

```
The camera slowly dollies back with a slight rightward drift. Glass panels fracture and drift apart, accelerating slightly. Red and amber caustic light bleeds between fragments, intensifying. Panels dim momentarily in sequence. Intersecting lines between panels break and disconnect. Smooth camera. 8 seconds.
```

## Assembly & Compositing Notes

1. **Generate clips in scene order** (1→9) for visual consistency
2. **Trim each clip** to exact duration: Scenes 1-2 (8s each), Scenes 3-7 (4-5s each), Scene 8 (10s), Scene 9 (8s)
3. **Color-match** across all clips — Scene 1 establishes the base temperature
4. **Transitions**: Cross-dissolve (8-12 frames) between scenes, except Scene 7→8 (snap zoom out)
5. **Text compositing** in video editor (DaVinci Resolve / Premiere / CapCut):
   - Engine labels in Inter Semi Bold + engine-colored glow
   - Data numbers in JetBrains Mono
   - Final tagline + logo in Inter Extra Bold
6. **Timeline**: 30fps, 1920x1080, total 1800 frames (60 seconds)
