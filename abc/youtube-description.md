## Description

Your finances live across five institutions. None of them talk to each other.

Poseidon is the AI-native coordination layer — connecting fragmented financial data into a single command center with four intelligent engines: Protect, Grow, Execute, and Govern. Every decision explained. Every action yours to approve.

This concept video was produced for the MIT CTO Program (Group 7) Impact Project, demonstrating Poseidon's vision for trusted, AI-Native financial platform.

—

What is Poseidon?
An AI-native money platform that unifies your finance by detecting anomalies, optimizing growth, automating execution, and ensuring compliance. All with full transparency and human-in-the-loop approval.

The Problem
$12.5B lost to fraud annually. $6B in Overdraft / Non-Sufficient-Fund fees. $133/month recurring payments wasted per household. Financial data is aggregated — but coordination is not.

The Solution
Four engines working as one:
• PROTECT: Anomaly detection and resolution
• GROW: AI-driven cashflow and portofolio recommendations
• EXECUTE: Automated action with human-in-the-loop
• GOVERN: 100% auditability of every AI decision

The Opportunity
4.3× value-to-cost ratio for customer. $17.5 trillion in addressable assets with minimum APY
